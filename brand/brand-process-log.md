## Brief

### Audience and positioning

Svif is a **Project orchestration product** coordinating durable Project continuity, execution surfaces, and capability providers. The current primary ChatGPT audience is individual/personal users, while Codex and other compatible Agent environments are active development/validation surfaces.

The brand should communicate controlled motion across replaceable execution environments without implying that any one execution surface owns the Project. It should feel like infrastructure/product identity rather than a generic AI assistant identity.

Svif and Agnir are independent Projects with a deliberate brand relationship. Svif represents motion, suspension, hovering/gliding, and orchestration around persistent Project continuity. Agnir represents the small durable pieces of Project truth. Their identities should be visibly related without making Agnir look like a subordinate Svif module.

### Intended surfaces

Exploration-stage targets:

- repository / GitHub identity;
- Plugin directory/listing identity;
- documentation and README branding;
- future favicon/application icon surfaces if and when a product surface requires them;
- future social/share assets only when actual metadata or distribution surfaces require them.

No production integration is authorized merely by starting exploration. README, Plugin metadata, favicon/PWA assets, and other shared production surfaces remain unchanged until a direction and master are explicitly approved.

### Required languages

- Brand mark: language-neutral where practical.
- Wordmark/product name: Latin-script `Svif` with current casing preserved unless explicitly changed later.
- Supporting documentation: English and Simplified Chinese remain parallel repository entry points.

### Constraints and exclusions

- Preserve the established meaning of `Svif`: Icelandic senses around flight, hovering, floating, and gliding; the particulate association comes from compounds such as `svifryk` and must not be misrepresented as the standalone word meaning “dust”.
- Preserve product neutrality: the identity must not imply permanent ownership by ChatGPT, GitHub, Cloudflare, a particular Agent, storage backend, or other replaceable execution/provider environment.
- Avoid generic AI-category clichés as the primary concept: sparkle/starburst, chatbot bubble, robot head, neural-brain icon, generic cloud, or ungrounded “AI magic” motifs.
- Favor vector-friendly geometry suitable for editable production SVG.
- The mark must remain distinguishable at small sizes; any small-size simplification requires separate explicit approval.
- Concept previews are not approved masters.
- Similarity review may identify risk but is not trademark/legal clearance.

### Success criteria

A successful Svif identity should:

1. express motion/orchestration without implying instability or loss of Project truth;
2. have a clear family relationship with Agnir while remaining independently recognizable;
3. avoid dependence on a specific execution surface/provider visual language;
4. support a simple vector master and reliable monochrome/contrast treatment;
5. remain legible and recognizable at repository/plugin-icon sizes;
6. provide enough geometric discipline that future production derivatives can be generated from one approved master rather than redrawn independently.
