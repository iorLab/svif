# Svif name-origin verification — 2026-09-01

This evidence records the linguistic basis and Principal-approved product mapping for the project name `Svif`.

## Linguistic basis

- Icelandic `svif` is a neuter noun with dictionary senses including flight, hovering, floating, and gliding. A secondary sense is plankton, and another is swinging/veering.
- The lexical element `svif` also appears in Icelandic `svifryk`, a term used in current Icelandic environmental material for particulate matter / suspended particulate matter, including PM10 and PM2.5.
- This was cross-checked on 2026-09-01 against:
  - Wiktionary Icelandic entry for `svif`: https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/svif
  - Umhverfis- og orkustofnun (Environment and Energy Agency of Iceland), air-quality material using `svifryk` for PM10 and PM2.5: https://uos.is/umhverfi/loft/loftgaedi
  - Umhverfisstofnun material explicitly listing `Svifryk – TSP, PM10, og PM2,5 (total suspended particulate og particulate matter)`: https://www.ust.is/loft/losun-loftmengunarefna/log-og-reglur/

These sources support the lexical/usage claims only. They do not define Svif product semantics. In particular, `svif` by itself is not recorded here as meaning dust or particulate matter; the particulate association comes from the compound/term `svifryk`.

## Product mapping

The Principal approved mapping the motion/suspension sense to Svif's existing Project-orchestration model: Project execution can move across replaceable Execution Surfaces and Capability Providers while durable Project truth remains owned by the Project rather than by any one execution environment.

The pairing with Agnir is intentionally conceptual: Agnir's name maps to the small durable pieces of Project truth; Svif's name maps to motion around that persistent continuity as execution environments change. This naming relationship is not a protocol dependency, storage-layout requirement, or claim that Agnir records physically move on every Svif operation.
