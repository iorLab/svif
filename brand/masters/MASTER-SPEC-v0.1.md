# Svif Logo Master Candidate v0.1

Status: **production master candidate — not locked**

This document converts the selected 01 + 03 + 05 concept direction into deterministic vector rules. It does not yet authorize README, Plugin, favicon, PWA, social, or other production integration.

## Identity invariant

- Role: **Motion Layer / 流动层**.
- Core form: flowing `S` ribbon plus particle trail.
- Family language: particles + geometry shared with Agnir.
- The mark must communicate motion/orchestration without implying that an execution surface owns Project truth.

## Candidate palette

These values were sampled from the Principal-selected concept board and are now the deterministic **v0.1 reconstruction palette**, not yet the locked final palette.

## Primary mark geometry

Source: `svif-mark-v0.1.svg`

- ViewBox: `0 0 512 512`.
- Shared S centerline:
  `M360 116 C300 72 200 68 145 112 C93 154 110 207 211 226 C322 247 392 278 370 341 C346 411 222 434 145 383`
- Ribbon layers use the same centerline so derivative geometry cannot drift independently.
- Candidate stroke widths: `104`, `72`, `34` units.
- Particle circles are decorative motion evidence around the stable S silhouette; they are not permitted to change the underlying S centerline.
- SVG is self-contained and has no external image, stylesheet, or font dependency.

## Small-size candidate

Source: `svif-mark-small-v0.1.svg`

The full particle treatment loses meaningful detail as the icon approaches favicon scale. The small-size candidate therefore preserves the exact S centerline but removes particles and reduces the mark to two ribbon strokes.

Preliminary QA recommendation, pending Principal approval:

- `64px+`: primary mark may be used.
- `32px`: small-size variant preferred.
- `16px`: small-size variant required.

This is a separate approved-variant candidate, not permission to simplify the primary master silently.
